Materials & specifications

Specialty work spans a lot of materials — domed resin, cut vinyl, magnetic stock, and exotic films. We'll recommend the right build for the look and the lifespan you need. A few of the systems we work in:

01 — Domed decals

3D resin badges

Dome
Optically clear polyurethane resin — flexible, self-healing, resists yellowing & scratching
Base
Cast or metallic vinyl with high-bond acrylic adhesive to stop edge-lift under dome tension
Look
3D embossed lens effect that amplifies color & metallics — premium product & vehicle badging
02 — RTA lettering

Ready-to-apply vinyl

Film
Color-through cast vinyl — embedded color, not printed
Delivery
Pre-spaced & pre-masked with transfer tape for aligned DIY or pro install
Look & life
No background — crisp painted-on letters · up to 8–10 yr outdoors
03 — Magnetic signs

Heavy-duty vehicle magnets

Stock
30 mil flexible magnetic sheeting — min. 85 lbs/sq ft pull, holds at highway speed
Backing
Anti-marring coating protects clear coat & factory paint from friction & moisture
Finish
UV-stable inks, outdoor laminate, rounded die-cut corners against wind lift
04 — Specialty finishes

Exotic & custom substrates

Metallic
Brushed aluminum, gold leaf, carbon fiber & multi-chrome color-shift films
High-vis
ASTM photoluminescent glow & ultra-bright fluorescent vinyls
Removable
7–8 mil static cling & microsuction for residue-free, temporary glass graphics
