Materials & specifications

The right decal comes down to the surface it lives on and how long it needs to last. From high-volume promo stickers to armored equipment labels, we spec substrate, adhesive, and finish to the job — a few of the systems we build with:

01 — Promotional & product

Stickers & labels

Material
3–4 mil monomeric calendered vinyl or high-gloss BOPP — premium hand-feel, tear-resistant
Adhesive
Permanent acrylic that bonds to glass, plastic, metal & cardboard without edge-curl
Finish
Matte or gloss UV-shield laminate · die-cut, kiss-cut sheets, or rolls
02 — High-tack & industrial

Equipment decals

Adhesion
Heavy-duty solvent-based acrylic for low-surface-energy substrates — powder coat, textured plastics
Substrate
10–20 mil armored vinyl or polycarbonate for impact & abrasion defense
Resistance
Immune to washdowns, grease & hydraulic fluid · -40°F to 300°F
03 — Specialty & security

Tamper, metallic & cling

Security
Destructible tamper-evident vinyl & silver polyester films that leave a permanent VOID pattern
Metallic
Super-cast metallic, brushed chrome & holographic films, top-coated for clean ink adhesion
Removable
7–8 mil static cling (no adhesive) & ultra-removable microsuction for residue-free glass

Not sure which applies? Tell us the surface, the environment, and how long it needs to last — we'll recommend the right build.
