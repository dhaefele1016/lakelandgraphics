Materials & specifications

Forecourt graphics face corrosive fuel vapors, raw-fuel drips, relentless UV, and around-the-clock illumination. Where it counts, we run Lakeland Graphics custom-formulated films and inks — tuned in-house for extended useful life so the reds, oranges, and yellows that fade first on lesser overlays are exactly where we outlast everyone. A few of the systems we build with:

01 — Main ID & pylon

Structures & price displays

Structure
Fabricated structural steel & marine-grade .063–.125 aluminum plate, finished in certified polyurethane
Sign faces
UV-stabilized polycarbonate / flexible faces for even light & wind load
Price displays
Outdoor LED with auto-dimming photocells, rated 100,000+ hrs continuous

Valances & pump graphics
