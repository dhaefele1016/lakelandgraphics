The details

What to expect along the way.

The short version — open whichever applies to you.

A successful job starts with good inputs. Two things help most:

  • A real photo of the current pump, panel, or surface — especially for replacement work. A quick photo settles exactly which decals you need.
  • Your artwork, if you have it — print-ready files give you the most control. Need design help? Just ask (extensive custom design is billed separately).
  • Custom jobs: about 5–10 business days, plus shipping — often sooner.
  • Stock items: ship the next business day, based on availability.

Need it sooner? Let us know, and we'll work with you to meet your timeline.

Every project is reviewed and proofed before production, and approved artwork is kept on file for easy reordering.
