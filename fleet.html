Materials & specifications

We match film, laminate, and adhesive to the surface and how long the graphic needs to last — painted metal, curved panels, glass, textured wall, or floor. A few of the systems we build with:

01 — Vehicle wraps

Commercial & color-change

Film
Premium dual-cast wrapping vinyl from industry-leading film makers
Laminate
Optically clear cast overlaminate in gloss, satin, or matte
Adhesive
Repositionable acrylic with air-egress channels for a bubble-free, damage-free removal
Build & life
3.5–4.5 mil total · 5–7 yr outdoor durability
02 — Fleet graphics

Trucks, vans & trailers

Film
Cast vinyl for curved vans; polymeric calendered for flat box trucks and trailers
Reflective
High-intensity reflective film — ASTM D4956 engineering-grade & prismatic
Adhesive
High-tack opaque blockout to mask existing graphics; resists power washing
Ink
UV-curable / latex for consistent color match across a whole fleet
03 — Environmental

Walls, glass & floor

Walls
Low-VOC wall vinyl for drywall; conformable film for brick, stucco & concrete
Glass
Perforated window film (60/40, 50/50), opti-clear, and etched / frosted crystal vinyls
Floor
Slip-resistant floor vinyl with UL 410 certified non-slip laminate
Indoor air
GREENGUARD Gold certified inks for schools, hospitals & offices
