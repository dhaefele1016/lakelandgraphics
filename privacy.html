Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Lakeland Graphics respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and what we do with it.

Who we are

Lakeland Graphics is a commercial printing company located at 9444 Deerwood Lane N, Maple Grove, Minnesota. You can reach us at 800.495.8107 or sales@lakelandgraphics.com.

Information we collect

Information you give us

When you submit a quote request, we collect the information you enter in the form:

  • Your name and company name
  • Your email address and phone number
  • The product category you're interested in and any description of your application
  • Any photos or artwork files you choose to upload

We only ask for what we need to prepare an accurate quote. You don't need to create an account to contact us.

Information collected automatically

Our web host records standard server logs, which may include your IP address, browser type, the pages you visited, and the date and time of your visit. These logs are used for security and to keep the site running properly. We do not use them to build a profile of you.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your quote request and prepare a quote
  • To communicate with you about your project or order
  • To keep records of quotes and orders as part of normal business operations
  • To protect the site against spam and abuse

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not use your information for advertising, and we will not add you to a marketing list without your permission.

How your information is shared

We share your information only where it's necessary to do business:

  • Our web host and email provider — the servers that deliver this website and carry your form submission to our inbox.
  • Suppliers and shipping carriers — only where needed to produce or deliver your order.
  • When required by law — if we're legally obligated to disclose information, or to protect our rights.

Artwork and uploaded files

Files you upload are sent to us by email and stored with your project record. Your artwork remains yours. We treat customer artwork as confidential and do not share it or use it in our own marketing without your permission.

How long we keep information

We keep quote requests and project records for as long as needed to serve you and to meet our legal, tax, and accounting obligations. If you'd like us to delete your information, contact us and we'll do so where we're not required to retain it.

Security

This site is served over an encrypted connection, and form submissions are transmitted securely. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect the information you send us.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies, and it does not track you across other websites. Your browser may store a small preference locally so the site remembers display settings between visits — this stays on your device and is never sent to us. See our note on cookies at the bottom of this page.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for business customers. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email sales@lakelandgraphics.com and we'll respond promptly. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state or national privacy law.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we'll change the "last updated" date above. Material changes will be noted on this page.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Call 800.495.8107 or email sales@lakelandgraphics.com.

A note for your attorney: this policy accurately describes how the website currently works, but it is a starting draft, not legal advice. Have your counsel review it before launch — particularly if you later add analytics, advertising pixels, or a customer pay portal that collects payment information.
